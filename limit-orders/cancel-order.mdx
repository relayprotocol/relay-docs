---
title: "Cancel an Order"
description: "Cancel a limit order with a gasless soft cancel or an onchain Permit2 nonce invalidation."
openapi: post /orders/{orderId}/cancel
hidden: true
---

A cancelled order closes with `outcome: "cancelled"` and `cancellationReason: "requested"`, and Relay never uses its signature.

**Which orders can be cancelled:**

- `pending` orders, and `open` orders that are waiting for their price, close at once.
- A `processing` order that Relay is retrying after a failed fill is refunded first. It stays `processing` and closes as `cancelled` once the refund lands, with the amount in `refundedAmount`.
- Anything else returns `409 ORDER_NOT_CANCELLABLE`: an order whose funds are being pulled, filled, or refunded, a `requires_action` order, an order past `expiresAt`, and a closed order.

With `onchain: true`, the order also returns a `cancel` transaction step that burns the permit's Permit2 nonce. It appears only while the permit is still valid and unused, and stays `incomplete` because Relay doesn't track whether you sent it. Choose soft or onchain on the first call: a second cancel returns `409 ORDER_ALREADY_CANCELLED`.

See [Cancel an order](/limit-orders/guide#cancel-an-order) in the integration guide for examples.
