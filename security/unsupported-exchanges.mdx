title: "Unsupported Exchanges"
description: "Exchanges that are not supported by Relay"
---

The Relay platform does not support transfers involving the exchanges below. This list is not exhaustive and can change as screening data and the platform blocklist are updated.

- @CryptoBot
- @wallet
- Bit Pin
- BitcoinVN
- Bitnest
- ChangeHero
- ChangeNOW
- CoinEx
- Crypto HK
- Cryptomus
- FixedFloat
- Heleket
- HTX (Huobi Global)
- Kraken Market
- N.Exchange
- Sideshift.ai
- Tornado.cash
- Wallex.ir
- Exmo.me

### What this means for you

- **Withdrawals from a blocked exchange:** Do not withdraw funds from a blocked exchange directly to a Relay deposit address. The deposit will be blocked and the transaction cannot be completed.
- **Sending to a blocked exchange:** Quotes where the recipient address is associated with a blocked exchange will be rejected with a `BLOCKED_WALLET_ADDRESS` error.

### Our policy

Blocking decisions use risk and sanctions databases together with the platform's internal blocklist. The platform does not make case-by-case exceptions for sanctioned entities. See [Transaction Screening](/security/compliance) for details.

For additional information, contact us via [Support](/resources/support).
