When funds held on the Relay Hub belong to a Safe (multisig) rather than a regular wallet, you can still withdraw them through [relay.link/withdraw](https://relay.link/withdraw) — the Safe signs the withdrawal authorization through the Safe app's message flow instead of a direct wallet signature. This guide walks through the full flow.

<Info>
This flow was verified with a 1-of-1 Safe on an EVM chain. Safes with more owners follow the same steps with extra confirmations, noted below. For the API-driven flow with a regular wallet, see [Programmatic Withdrawals](/references/protocol/guides/withdrawals).

## Before you start

- Open your Safe at [app.safe.global](https://app.safe.global) on the chain where you want to receive funds.
- Have a signer wallet (for example MetaMask) that is an owner of the Safe, unlocked and set to that same chain.
- Keep two browser tabs open side by side: the Safe app and [relay.link/withdraw](https://relay.link/withdraw). You will switch between them several times.
- Enable **blind signing** in the Safe settings. The withdrawal authorization is a raw hash, and the Safe cannot display its contents.
- The Safe itself needs no ETH. Gas for the final transaction is paid by the signer wallet.

## Connect the Safe to Relay

1. **Connect your signer wallet to the Safe app.** In the Safe app, click **Connect Wallet** and pick your signer wallet. The account shown in the top right must be one of the Safe owners.
2. **Choose Safe as your wallet on relay.link/withdraw.** Open [relay.link/withdraw](https://relay.link/withdraw), click **Connect**, and pick **Safe** in the wallet list. Do not pick your signer wallet here — Relay would look up the signer's balance instead of the Safe's.
3. **Pair through WalletConnect.** Relay shows a QR code. Click **Copy QR URI**, switch to the Safe app, open the WalletConnect icon in the top bar, and paste the URI. Relay should now show the Safe address in its top right corner.

## Sign the withdrawal authorization

4. **Fill in the withdrawal.** In Relay, open the **Withdraw** tab and select the chain and token. The **Withdraw to Address** field defaults to the connected Safe, which is what you want. Check the available balance, then click **Withdraw**.
5. **Confirm the message in the Safe app.** A **Confirm message** modal appears containing a 32-byte hash — the withdrawal authorization Relay asked your Safe to sign. The blind-signing warning is Safe's standard notice for any raw hash and is expected here. Keep this modal open until every required owner has confirmed; closing it aborts the request and you must restart from step 4.
6. **Switch network if prompted, then sign.** If a **Change your wallet network** banner appears, switch and approve in your signer wallet — the **Sign** button only activates once the signer is on the right network.
7. **Reach the signature threshold.** The Safe **Messages** tab lists the message as **Confirmed** once enough owners have signed. For thresholds above 1, each additional owner opens their own Safe app session, goes to **Transactions → Messages**, opens the message, and clicks **Sign**. The Relay tab keeps waiting until the threshold is reached.

## Submit the on-chain transaction

8. **Submit from Relay.** Once the signature is collected, the Relay progress bar moves to **Submit**. Click **Submit Transaction**. The prepared withdrawal expires about one hour after preparation; if it expires, the funds return to your hub balance and you can start again.
9. **Review the transaction in the Safe app.** A **Confirm transaction** modal appears. Before signing, check three things: the call is **execute** on **RelayDepository**, the first address inside the request is your Safe address, and the amount matches what you entered in Relay. Then sign and approve in your signer wallet.
10. **Execute from the queue if needed.** If the transaction lands in the Safe **Queue** instead of executing immediately, open the Queue tab and click **Execute** on the RelayDepository entry. For thresholds above 1, the other owners confirm here first; any owner can then execute.

## Confirm the funds arrived

The Safe shows **Transaction was successful** with the transaction hash, and the block explorer's internal transactions show the transfer from the Relay Depository to your Safe address.

## If the signing prompt never appears

- **Wrong network on the signer wallet.** The Safe **Sign** button stays disabled until the wallet is on the Safe's chain.
- **Connected wallet is not an owner.** Check the connected account against the Safe's owner list under Settings.
- **Threshold above 1.** The first owner signs in the modal; the others sign from **Transactions → Messages** in their own Safe app session while the Relay tab stays open.
- **Modal was closed.** Closing the Confirm message modal aborts the request. Start again from step 4.
- **Relay connected to the signer wallet instead of the Safe.** Disconnect in Relay and reconnect choosing Safe. The **Withdraw to Address** field must show the Safe address.
- **Browser blocking pop-ups.** Allow pop-ups for app.safe.global so the signer wallet can open.
