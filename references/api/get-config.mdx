<Warning>
  `GET /config` and `GET /config/v2` are **deprecated as of October 30, 2026** and retire with no successor on **January 28, 2027**. From December 14, 2026 their rate limits are reduced in stages. Use [`GET /chains/liquidity`](/references/api/get-chains-liquidity) for solver liquidity per chain, and [`POST /quote/v2`](/references/api/get-quote-v2) for the executable amount of a request.
</Warning>
