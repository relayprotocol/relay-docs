---
title: What is Relay?
description: "Enterprise-grade onchain payments infrastructure"
---

Relay is payments infrastructure that brings the convenience and confidence of fiat rails onchain. Integrate once to reach every chain, with execution enterprises can rely on and the tooling to build the experience your users expect.

[Create an account →](https://dashboard.relay.link)

## Major use cases

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Accept onchain payments" icon="credit-card" href="/solutions/payment-service-providers">
    Let customers pay from supported chains and settle in the asset your application uses.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Move assets across chains" icon="link" href="/use-cases/bridging">
    Bridge assets between supported chains through one transaction flow.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Add swaps" icon="repeat" href="/use-cases/cross-chain-swaps">
    Exchange assets on the same chain or across different chains.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Execute crosschain calls" icon="hexagon-nodes" href="/use-cases/calling">
    Let users take an action on one chain and pay with assets from another.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
